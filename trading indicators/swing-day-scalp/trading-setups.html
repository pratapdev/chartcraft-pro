Trend Template — ALL 8 must pass

VCP Requirements
  • Minimum 2 contractions (3 is ideal)
  • Each contraction range narrows (T1 > T2 > T3)
  • Volume dries up each contraction
  • Final squeeze: ultra-tight range + near-zero vol
  • Right side closer to 52wk high than left side

  • Revenue growth ≥ 20%
  • Expanding profit margins
  • Institutional accumulation rising
  • Stock in a top-performing sector

Entry — Exact Trigger
Buy at the pivot breakout: price clears the highest resistance of the base on volume ≥ 1.5× average. Use a buy-stop 0.05–0.10 above pivot high. Can enter intraday — do NOT wait for the daily close. Do NOT buy inside the base. If you missed the breakout, wait for the first pullback.
Stop Loss — Hard Rule
Maximum 7–8% below entry. For tight VCPs, stop can be 3–5% below base low. Never lower the stop. Size position so max loss = 0.5–1% of total account. If stopped out, the setup is invalidated — re-evaluate fresh.
Exit — Profit Taking

  • Trail stop on rest using the 50 MA
  • Sell into strength on climax volume (wide-range day)
  • Full exit if closes below 50 MA on heavy volume
  • Reduce to 1/2 size before earnings — never full size

Pre-Market Checklist (before 9:30)
  • Stock has a catalyst (news, earnings beat, upgrade)
  • Pre-market gap ≥ 1% from prior close
  • Pre-market volume ≥ 2× normal
  • Daily chart: above 20 MA = long bias, below = short bias
  • No major resistance immediately above the gap area
  • Max 5–6 names on watchlist — no more
Opening Range Formation (9:30–10:00)
Wait for the first 30 minutes (6 candles on 5-min) to close. Mark the Opening Range High (ORH) and Opening Range Low (ORL). Do NOT trade during this window. Just observe. Note the VWAP and whether price respects it. A narrow OR = stronger breakout potential.
Entry — Exact Trigger
Enter when a 5-min candle closes above ORH (long) or below ORL (short). Candle must close in upper third of its range. Volume on that candle must be ≥ 1.5× the opening range average. VWAP must be below price for longs. Order: buy-stop 1¢ above breakout candle high.
Stop Loss
Initial stop at ORL for longs, ORH for shorts. On wide-range days: use 50% of OR width as stop (tighter). If price re-enters the opening range after breaking out → exit immediately, no waiting. Max risk per trade = 1% of account. After 2 losing ORBs in one day → stop trading for the day.
Exit Strategy
  • Scale 50% at 1× opening range size above ORH (2:1 R:R)
  • Trail remainder with 9 EMA on 5-min chart
  • Hard exit: if no clear trend by 11:30am EST, close
  • Never hold ORB through lunch (12–1pm EST)
  • Close all positions by 3:45pm EST if still open
Market Conditions Filter
Check SPY/QQQ premarket direction. Long ORBs only on gap-up, bullish broad market. Short ORBs only on gap-down, bearish broad market. Choppy flat pre-market = skip or half-size. Best days: Tuesday–Thursday. Worst days: Monday (weekend noise), Friday (positioning). Earnings season = highest win rate for ORB.
✓ Do This
  • Build and finalize watchlist before 9:15am
  • Let all 6 range candles fully close before acting
  • Confirm the breakout candle's volume spike
  • Use VWAP position as your directional filter
  • Stop after 2 losses — don't revenge trade
  • Scale out at first target, trail the rest
✗ Never Do This
  • Trade ORB on no-catalyst, low-volume stocks
  • Enter on candle 1 or 2 — range is not formed yet
  • Hold through the 12–1pm lunch chop
  • Ignore daily-chart resistance directly above ORH
  • Chase the breakout candle after it's already run
  • Take a long ORB against a gap-down bear market day

Why This Setup Works
Institutions use VWAP as their execution benchmark. Their algorithms are programmed to buy below VWAP and sell above it. When price deviates ≥1.5% from VWAP on volume exhaustion, you're fading a retail-driven spike into institutional supply/demand — the highest-probability fade in the market.
Setup Conditions — All Required
  • Stock is in-play (news, gap, high relative volume)
  • Price moves ≥ 1.5% from VWAP in one direction
  • Volume spike on the extension (exhaustion candle)
  • 5-min chart: NOT strongly one-directional trending
  • VWAP and 9 EMA visible and not too far apart
Entry — Exact Trigger
After the extension spike, wait for a reversal candle to form on the 1-min chart (e.g., bearish engulf or doji with long top wick for short). Volume on the reversal candle should be lower than the spike. Enter 1¢ below the reversal candle's low (short) or above its high (long). Never enter on the extension candle itself.
Stop Loss
Stop goes above the extension candle's high + 5¢ buffer (for shorts), or below its low (for longs). This is the structural invalidation point. Keep stops tight: typically 0.2–0.4%. If you need a stop wider than 0.5% to make it work, the setup isn't clean — skip it.
Exit Strategy
  • Target 1: 9 EMA — take 50% off the table
  • Target 2: VWAP itself — close the rest
  • If stalls at 9 EMA with no momentum → exit all
  • Time-stop: 5 minutes max — if not moving, exit
  • Max hold: never into lunch hour or session close
Session, Risk & Discipline
  • Best windows: 9:30–11:30am and 2–3:30pm EST
  • Avoid: 12–1:30pm (lunch, low volume, no edge)
  • Max risk per trade: 0.25% of account
  • Daily loss limit: 1% — then stop
  • After 3 consecutive losses → done for the day
  • Max 10–15 setups/day — quality over quantity
✓ Do This
  • Require BOTH VWAP extension AND volume exhaustion
  • Wait for the reversal candle to fully close
  • Take partial profit at the 9 EMA every time
  • Use direct-access execution (DAS Trader, Lightspeed)
  • Track each trade — know your win rate by condition
  • Stop after 3 losses — emotions destroy scalpers
✗ Never Do This
  • Chase the extension candle trying to catch the top
  • Scalp against a strong trending stock (fade only in ranges)
  • Trade during 12–1:30pm lunch chop
  • Widen stop after entry — respect the thesis or exit
  • Trade more than 15–20 scalps per day
  • Scalp stocks with spreads wider than 1–2 cents
