Kill Zone Backgrounds
Blue = London Open (07:00–10:00 UTC)
Orange = NY Open (12:00–15:00 UTC)

Only trade during these windows. Signals outside are ignored.

Order Blocks (OB)
Green box = Bullish OB — last bearish candle before a bullish impulse.
Red box = Bearish OB — last bullish candle before a bearish impulse.
Dashed midline at 50%. Box fades (grey) when price trades through it = mitigated.
⬜
Fair Value Gaps (FVG)
Teal = Bullish FVG — gap between high[2] and low[0].
Amber = Bearish FVG — gap between low[2] and high[0].
Auto-removed when price fills the gap. These are magnets — price is drawn back to fill them.
💧
Liquidity Levels (BSL / SSL)
BSL ▲ = Buy-Side Liquidity — resting stops above swing highs.
SSL ▼ = Sell-Side Liquidity — resting stops below swing lows.
Institutions hunt these before reversing. A sweep of these levels is a setup trigger.
🏗
BOS & CHoCH Labels
BOS ▲ / ▼ = Break of Structure (continuation). Price closes beyond the most recent swing high or low. Confirms the current bias.

⚡ CHoCH = Change of Character (reversal). BOS against the prior structural bias. Yellow label = highest-priority signal.
📐
OTE Zone Lines
Dashed yellow lines = Optimal Trade Entry zone (61.8%–79% Fibonacci of the last 20-bar swing). Only shown when Killzone + OTE Entry is selected and a Kill Zone is active. Enter at the 79% level for lowest risk.
🎯
Entry Labels + SL/TP Lines
🟢 LONG ENTRY label fires below the bar when all conditions align.
🔴 SHORT ENTRY label fires above the bar.
Each label shows Entry / SL / TP1 / TP2 prices and R:R. Dashed lines extend 55 bars right: red=SL, yellow=TP1, green=TP2.

01 Order Block + FVG ▼
The core ICT model. Price returns to an OB that also contains a FVG during a Kill Zone. Highest-probability prop firm setup.
Entry Checklist
  • Daily/4H bias established first — don't trade against HTF trend
  • Price inside London or NY Kill Zone (blue/orange background)
  • Price retraces into a green/red OB box on the chart
  • A FVG (teal or amber box) must exist inside or near the OB
  • Entry at OB midline (dashed line) on a confirmation candle
  • Candle direction must match structural bias (BOS ▲ for longs)
What to look for
  • OB is unmitigated (still bright green/red, not faded grey)
  • Most recent BOS/CHoCH label confirms same direction as trade
  • No liquidity (BSL/SSL) sitting between entry and TP
  • Volume is ideally declining into the OB (no panic selling into support)
STOP LOSS
Below/above the Order Block (not just the wick — the full box)
TAKE PROFIT
TP1 at 50% to next liquidity · TP2 at BSL/SSL level (full 1:3 R:R)
15m setup 5m entry 1m confirm Avoid 1H+ entry TF
02 Killzone + OTE Entry ▼
Uses the 61.8–79% Fibonacci retracement of the last impulse leg. Yellow OTE zone lines appear on chart during Kill Zones.
Entry Checklist
  • Identify the most recent impulse swing (20-bar high to low or v/v)
  • Price must retrace into the yellow OTE zone (61.8–79% Fib)
  • Must be inside a Kill Zone background on the chart
  • Enter at the 79% level (deepest retracement, best R:R)
  • Trigger: CHoCH or reversal candle at the 79% level
Common mistakes
  • Entering at 61.8% instead of 79% — worse R:R, higher fail rate
  • Trading OTE outside Kill Zones — zone loses institutional validity
  • Not confirming with LTF CHoCH before entry
  • Taking the trade when OB is mitigated (faded) near OTE level
STOP LOSS
Beyond the extreme of the swing (beyond the 100% level)
TAKE PROFIT
Previous swing high (BSL) for longs · Previous swing low (SSL) for shorts
15m structure 5m OTE 1m CHoCH trigger
03 Liquidity Sweep + CHoCH ▼
The most pure ICT setup. Wait for a BSL/SSL level to be swept, then a CHoCH confirms institutional reversal. ⚡ CHoCH label = trigger.
Exact sequence on chart
  • BSL ▲ or SSL ▼ dotted line is visible on chart
  • Price wicks beyond the dotted level (the sweep wick)
  • Candle closes back inside the prior range (fake-out confirmed)
  • Within 1–3 bars, a ⚡ CHoCH label fires on the chart
  • Enter on the retest of the CHoCH level after the reversal
Reading the sweep
  • The wick beyond SSL/BSL is the stop-hunt — institutions filling their orders
  • The close back inside = trapped retail traders who chased the breakout
  • CHoCH = institutions now pushing in the opposite direction
  • The retest is where you get a second chance entry with smaller SL
STOP LOSS
Beyond the sweep wick extreme (not the original level — beyond the wick)
TAKE PROFIT
Opposing BSL/SSL on HTF · Minimum 1:3 R:R required
15m sweep 5m CHoCH 1m retest entry Not valid without CHoCH
04 Silver Bullet (10–11 AM EST) ▼
60-minute window only (15:00–16:00 UTC). Same OB logic but constrained to the most powerful reversal window of the NY session.
Time-window discipline
  • Chart background turns purple (London Close) — this is the window
  • Identify AM session premium/discount on 15m chart first
  • Drop to 5m or 1m — look for a fresh OB or FVG
  • Bias = continuation of the AM session directional move
  • Signal label fires: enter at 1m OB during the window
  • Exit before 11 AM EST (16:00 UTC) — window closes
Why this window works
  • London and NY overlap creates peak institutional volume
  • Large London positions are squared or reversed in this window
  • Liquidity from AM session is used to fill opposing positions
  • This is ICT's most consistent time-of-day edge
STOP LOSS
5–10 pips behind the 1m OB that triggered entry
TAKE PROFIT
1:3 R:R minimum · Must hit before 11 AM EST or close at window end
5m bias 1m entry 60-min window only No pre-window entries
05 Breaker Block ▼
A failed Order Block — price broke through it, now it flips role and becomes a signal from the other side. Rarer but very clean setups.
Identifying a Breaker
  • Find an OB that price traded through (box will be faded/grey)
  • That zone is now a Breaker — it flips to the opposite side
  • Bullish Breaker: old bearish OB that was broken upward
  • Bearish Breaker: old bullish OB that was broken downward
  • Wait for price to return to the zone from the new side
  • Confirm with BOS/CHoCH label + Kill Zone active
Practical identification
  • A grey/faded OB box on chart = potential Breaker zone
  • Price must fully break out and pull back — not just wick through
  • The most powerful Breakers are at previous session highs/lows
  • Avoid Breakers that are too old — confluence with FVG makes them stronger
STOP LOSS
Back inside the Breaker zone (invalidation = it acts as OB again)
TAKE PROFIT
Next liquidity pool (BSL/SSL) in direction of trade · 1:3 R:R
1H Breaker ID 15m confirmation 5m entry Less frequent

01 VWAP Bounce ▼
Mean reversion from the ±2 SD band back toward VWAP. Best risk/reward of all 5 setups. TP is always VWAP.
Exact checklist
  • Price wicks into the ±2 SD band (green outer bands)
  • Candle must close back inside ±2 SD (rejection confirmed)
  • Volume bar must be bright (≥1.5× MA) — the fuel
  • RSI: below OB level for longs | above OS level for shorts
  • Session: green background (RTH Prime or Close only)
  • Enter at the close of signal candle
What kills this setup
  • Low volume wick — institutions aren't involved, bounce won't hold
  • Price at ±2 SD during a trending day — VWAP keeps moving away
  • News event just dropped — vol spike is news, not absorption
  • Entering on the first touch of ±2SD — wait for the close back inside
STOP LOSS
0.3 × ATR beyond the wick extreme (not the candle close)
TAKE PROFIT
VWAP line — mean reversion target. Close full position at VWAP.
1m entry 2m entry 5m context Avoid trending days
02 Opening Range Breakout (ORB) ▼
First 30 minutes sets the range. Break above OR High or below OR Low with high volume, then enter on the retest. Classic momentum play.
Step-by-step
  • During 9:30–10:00 AM ET, do not trade — let OR form
  • At 10:00 AM, cyan/orange box and lines appear on chart
  • Wait for a candle to close beyond OR High or Low
  • Volume must be high (bright bar) on the breakout candle
  • Best entry = retest of OR High/Low after breakout (label fires here)
  • Reject if price re-enters the range — setup is failed
Failure filters
  • No news catalyst for the gap/direction = weak ORB
  • Retest wicks back inside OR — do not enter
  • Gap-and-go opens with OR entirely above/below PDH/PDL — fades often beat ORBs in this context
  • Volume declining on retest candle = weak follow-through ahead
STOP LOSS
Back inside the Opening Range (invalidation level)
TAKE PROFIT
OR height projected from breakout point (auto-calculated in label)
5m ORB signal 1m–2m retest entry 9:45–11:30 AM ET only No afternoon ORBs
03 VWAP Trend Scalp ▼
Trade with the VWAP trend. Pull back to ±1 SD in trend direction, look for bounce continuation. Works on trending news days.
Checklist
  • Price firmly above VWAP (3+ consecutive closes for longs)
  • Price pulls back and touches the +1 SD band (blue)
  • Candle closes back above +1 SD (bounce confirmation)
  • RSI: between 40–70 (not overbought, not oversold extreme)
  • Enter on the close of the bounce candle at +1 SD
  • Volume on signal bar: average or above (not low)
Context requirement
  • Must be a trending day — VWAP angle ≥ 30° visually
  • Do not use on range days — use VWAP Bounce or Value Area instead
  • If price is at +1SD but VWAP is flattening — trend is ending, skip
  • Best after a macro catalyst (earnings, CPI, FOMC) creates a directional day
STOP LOSS
Beyond the ±1 SD band (if it breaks through, trend is pausing)
TAKE PROFIT
±2 SD extension or previous intraday swing high/low in trend direction
3m or 5m Trending news sessions Useless on range days
04 PDH / PDL Fade ▼
Previous day extremes are liquidity magnets. Price taps PDH or PDL at open, rejects, and targets PDC. Clean R:R. Pre-market is prime time.
Exact sequence
  • PDH/PDL dotted lines auto-appear at session open
  • Price wicks into PDH (short) or PDL (long)
  • Rejection candle closes away from the level (hammer at PDL / shooting star at PDH)
  • RSI confirms: OB (>60) at PDH or OS (<40) at PDL
  • Enter at close of rejection candle
  • TP = PDC (yellow dotted line) — auto-target in label
Edge statistics
  • PDC TP hit rate is ~75–80% on liquid instruments when RSI confirms
  • Works best on gap-up/gap-down opens where price immediately tests PDH/PDL
  • If price opens above PDH or below PDL — that's a different setup, not a fade
  • Strongest signal: PDH/PDL within ±1 SD of current VWAP level
STOP LOSS
0.3–0.5 × ATR beyond PDH or PDL (not the wick, beyond the level)
TAKE PROFIT
PDC (previous day close) — auto-calculated, shown in entry label
5m level read 1m–3m entry Pre-market + first 30 min RTH
05 VWAP Value Area Scalp ▼
Inside the ±1 SD zone, price rotates between the bands. Fade each touch of ±1 SD on low volume. Best on quiet, range-bound days.
Checklist
  • Confirm range day: price inside ±1 SD for 30+ minutes
  • Price tags -1 SD (blue band) from above → fade long
  • Price tags +1 SD (blue band) from below → fade short
  • Volume must be declining or average — not high
  • RSI filter active: OS for longs, OB for shorts
  • TP = VWAP midpoint or opposite ±1 SD
When NOT to use
  • Trending days — VWAP will drift, bands follow, no clean rotation
  • First 15 minutes of RTH — too volatile for range logic
  • After major news events — distribution is disrupted
  • When price is consistently above or below VWAP — not ranging
STOP LOSS
Beyond the ±1 SD band (break = range is over, exit immediately)
TAKE PROFIT
VWAP midpoint (50% of the range) — take partial profits here
1m or 2m Range-bound sessions Never on trending days Avoid news windows
Non-negotiable scalping rules
NEVER trade during lunch (12:00–1:30 PM ET). Volume drops 60–70%. Spreads widen. Algos run stops in both directions. Session filter blocks signals automatically — if you override it manually, expect fakeouts.
NEVER enter on low-volume bars. Volume colour on bars is not decoration. A grey bar at ±2 SD means retail selling into a vacuum — no institutional absorption, no bounce fuel.
Always set your daily max loss before the session opens. For scalping: 1.5% account max daily drawdown. Hit it → close platform. The edge only exists when you're disciplined enough to stop.
Use 1m–5m only. The panel TF checker tells you if you're on the wrong timeframe. On 15m+, setups are too slow — you're no longer scalping, you're swing trading with scalp risk management, which is a net negative.
